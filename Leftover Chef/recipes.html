Rice Recipes

Have day-old cold rice? These 3 Thai dishes turn it into perfection.

RICE · 15 MIN

Khao Pad (Thai Fried Rice)

Traditional Thai fried rice seasoned with fish sauce, soy sauce, and fresh lime.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked leftover jasmine rice (chilled)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • Lime wedges and cucumber slices for serving

How to make it

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté garlic until fragrant.
  2. Push garlic aside, crack in the egg, and scramble gently.
  3. Add cold leftover rice, breaking up any clumps.
  4. Drizzle fish sauce and soy sauce over the rice; stir-fry on high heat for 3 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with cucumber and fresh lime.

RICE · SOUP · 15 MIN

Khao Tom (Thai Rice Soup)

A comforting, fragrant broth cooked with leftover jasmine rice and fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp minced ginger
  • Chopped cilantro and green onion

How to make it

  1. Bring broth to a boil in a small pot.
  2. Add minced ginger, soy sauce, and leftover rice.
  3. Simmer for 5–8 minutes until the rice expands and softens.
  4. Ladle into a bowl and garnish with cilantro and green onions.

RICE · SWEET & SAVORY · 15 MIN

Khao Pad Sapparot (Pineapple Fried Rice)

A sweet and savory fried rice styled with leftover rice, curry powder, and pineapple chunks.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups leftover cooked rice
  • 1/2 cup pineapple chunks
  • 1/2 tsp yellow curry powder
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • Cashews or raisins (optional)

How to make it

  1. Heat oil in a wok or large pan.
  2. Add rice, curry powder, and soy sauce, mixing until yellow and aromatic.
  3. Toss in pineapple chunks and cook for another 2 minutes.
  4. Top with roasted cashews and serve warm.

Egg Recipes

Turn simple eggs into crispy, flavorful Thai favorite meals.

EGG · 10 MIN

Kai Jeow (Thai Omelette)

A crispy on the outside, fluffy on the inside Thai omelette served over warm rice.

Ingredients

  • 2–3 eggs
  • 1 tsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp lime juice or soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

How to make it

  1. Beat eggs vigorously with fish sauce and lime juice until frothy.
  2. Heat oil in a pan until very hot.
  3. Pour the egg mixture from high up to create crisp edges.
  4. Fry until golden brown, flip once, and serve over rice.

EGG · 5 MIN

Kai Dao (Crispy Thai Fried Egg)

Crispy browned egg whites with a soft yolk, perfect for topping any rice dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Pinch of chili flakes or soy sauce

How to make it

  1. Heat oil over medium-high heat.
  2. Crack the egg directly into hot oil.
  3. Spoon hot oil over the egg yolk until whites are crispy and bubbly.
  4. Remove and place over rice with soy sauce.

EGG · SWEET & TANGY · 15 MIN

Kai Louk Keuy (Son-in-Law Eggs)

Hard-boiled eggs fried till golden and drizzled with a tangy sweet tamarind sauce.

Ingredients

  • 3 hard-boiled eggs (peeled)
  • 1 tbsp tamarind paste or lime juice
  • 1 tbsp palm sugar or brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • Crispy fried shallots for garnish

How to make it

  1. Deep-fry peeled hard-boiled eggs until the outer skin turns golden crisp. Slice in half.
  2. In a small pan, simmer sugar, tamarind, and fish sauce into a thick glaze.
  3. Pour the sauce over the eggs and top with crispy fried shallots.

Chicken Recipes

Transform leftover chicken into savory, herb-packed Thai dishes.

CHICKEN · 15 MIN

Pad Kra Pao (Basil Stir-Fry)

Thailand's iconic street dish—spicy chicken stir-fried with garlic and fresh basil.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup shredded or chopped leftover chicken
  • 3 cloves garlic & 2 red chilies (pounded)
  • 1 cup fresh holy basil or sweet basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp soy sauce

How to make it

  1. Sauté pounded garlic and chilies in oil until fragrant.
  2. Add leftover chicken and toss until heated through.
  3. Season with oyster sauce and soy sauce.
  4. Turn off the heat, toss in fresh basil leaves, and stir until wilted.

CHICKEN · SALAD · 10 MIN

Larb Gai (Spicy Thai Chicken Salad)

A zesty, spicy chicken salad flavored with lime juice, chilies, and herbs.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked shredded chicken
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • Sliced red onion and mint leaves

How to make it

  1. Warm leftover chicken briefly in a pan with a splash of water.
  2. Transfer chicken to a bowl and let cool slightly.
  3. Mix in lime juice, fish sauce, red onion, and chili flakes.
  4. Toss with mint leaves and serve at room temperature.

CHICKEN · CASHEW · 15 MIN

Gai Pad Med Mamuang (Cashew Chicken)

Leftover chicken stir-fried with crunchy cashews, bell peppers, and savory sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked chicken
  • 1/4 cup roasted cashew nuts
  • 1/2 bell pepper or onion (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp chili paste or soy sauce

How to make it

  1. Heat oil in a wok and fry onions and bell peppers.
  2. Add cooked chicken and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  3. Stir in oyster sauce and chili paste.
  4. Fold in roasted cashew nuts and serve immediately.
