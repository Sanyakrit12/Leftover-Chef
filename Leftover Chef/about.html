OUR STORY & MISSION

About Leftover Chef

Reducing food waste, one delicious Thai meal at a time.

Why We Started

Every day, cooked rice, stray eggs, and leftover chicken sit in fridges around the world, eventually going to waste. At the same time, Thai cuisine is famous for being incredibly resourceful—turning simple, everyday leftovers into vibrant, mouth-watering street food classics like Khao Pad and Pad Kra Pao.

Leftover Chef was built to bridge that gap. We make it effortless to transform ordinary fridge leftovers into authentic, easy-to-cook Thai dishes without requiring complicated grocery runs or specialized chef skills.
